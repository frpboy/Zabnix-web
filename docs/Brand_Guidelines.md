# Brand Guidelines

This document outlines the brand voice, messaging guidelines, vocabulary standards, and visual aesthetics of the Zabnix corporate brand. All copywriters, designers, and developers must align with these guidelines to preserve the brand's identity.

---

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning

### Positioning Statement
Zabnix is an engineered technology company. We do not write sales pitches; we build production-grade platforms. Our brand is characterized by extreme technical depth, minimal decoration, and stark, premium developer platform styling.

### Core Values
* **Confidence**: Direct sentences showing capability and clarity.
* **Technical Depth**: Using proper technical terms (e.g. `microservices`, `Flutter codebase`, `Next.js App Router`) instead of abstract business phrases.
* **Modernity**: Clean, stark, Vercel-inspired white layouts supported by Level 3 shadows.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Guidelines

### Write in the Active, Direct Voice
* **Yes**: `We engineer custom software that speeds up operations.`
* **No**: `Custom software services will be engineered by Zabnix to streamline your business workflow.`

### Address the Reader Directly (Second Person)
* **Yes**: `Build faster. Automate smarter.`
* **No**: `Our clients build faster and automate smarter.`

---

## 3. Vocabulary Restrictions

To keep the brand mature and credible, we strictly avoid traditional marketing buzzwords and unverified self-proclamations.

| Avoid | Use Instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `world-class engineers`, `leading company`, `best-in-industry` | `premium product engineering firm`, `full-stack team` | Self-praise damages technical credibility. Let stats and case studies speak. |
| `synergy`, `disruptive`, `paradigm shift` | `automated workflows`, `unified portals` | Buzzwords sound generic and obscure what we actually build. |
| `utilize`, `leverage` | `use`, `build with` | Simple verbs are punchy and modern. |

---

## 4. Copy & Layout Rules

### Sentence-Case Display Headlines
* All display headings and page headlines must use standard sentence-casing:
  * **Correct**: `Everything your enterprise needs.`
  * **Incorrect**: `Everything Your Enterprise Needs`
  * **Incorrect**: `EVERYTHING YOUR ENTERPRISE NEEDS`

### Punctuation-Terminated Headings
* Display headers are terminated by a period (`.`). This punctuation anchors the stark statement visually.
  * **Correct**: `Build faster. Automate smarter.`
  * **Incorrect**: `Build faster. Automate smarter`

### Numerals Over Words
* When writing counts, lists, or metrics, prioritize numerals over written numbers to convey technical precision:
  * **Correct**: `4 countries`, `8 deployments`, `27 pages`
  * **Incorrect**: `four countries`, `eight deployments`, `twenty-seven pages`
